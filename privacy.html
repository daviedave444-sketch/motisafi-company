Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Motisafi sells vehicles in Kenya. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it. You can contact us at davisxm118@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the inquiry form, by email or by WhatsApp. This usually means your name, email address, the vehicle you are asking about and the contents of your message.

The inquiry form does not send data to our servers. It opens your email app with a message already filled in, and nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it loads fonts from your device rather than from a third party. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and technical purposes.

4. How we use your information

We use the information you send us to reply to your inquiry and to handle any sale or viewing that follows. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

5. Who else sees it

Your message is processed by the email provider or messaging service you use to contact us, such as your email provider and WhatsApp, under their own privacy policies. We share your information with others only when needed to complete a transaction you asked for, or when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to deal with your request and to keep records of any resulting sale. After that we delete them.

7. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask us for access to your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address above and we will respond. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
